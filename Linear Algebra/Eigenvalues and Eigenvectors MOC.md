# 🗺️ Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors MOC

## 📍 Overview
This Map of Content (MOC) serves as the central hub for the topic of **Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors**, a cornerstone of [[Linear Algebra]] in the [[GATE CS]] syllabus.

## 🔗 Core Concepts
1. [[Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors Definition]]: The "Why" and the fundamental equation $Ax = \lambda x$.
2. [[Characteristic Equation]]: The algebraic path to finding $\lambda$.
3. [[Properties of Eigenvalues]]: Trace, Determinant, and Sum/Product relationships (GATE favorite).
4. [[Eigenvalues of Special Matrices]]: Identity, Diagonal, Triangular, Symmetric, and Orthogonal matrices.
5. [[Eigenvectors and Eigenspaces]]: Finding the vectors and understanding [[Linear Independence]].
6. [[Diagonalization]]: Transforming a matrix into a diagonal form.
7. [[Cayley-Hamilton Theorem]]: Every matrix satisfies its own characteristic equation.

## 🚀 Advanced & Applied Topics
- [[Algebraic and Geometric Multiplicity]]: Deep dive into the structure of eigenvalues.
- [[Power of a Matrix]]: Using eigenvalues for $A^k$.
- [[Applications of Eigenvalues]]: Connections to [[Markov Chains]], [[Graph Theory]] (Spectral Clustering), and [[Principal Component Analysis]].

## 🎯 Quick Revision
- **Sum of Eigenvalues** = [[Trace]] of Matrix.
- **Product of Eigenvalues** = [[Determinant]] of Matrix.
- **Distinct Eigenvalues** $\implies$ Linearly Independent Eigenvectors.
- **Cayley-Hamilton** is used to find $A^{-1}$ and $A^n$.
